# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 19 October 2026**

DoDoDoodle is designed for children with privacy at its core.

## Your Privacy

- **No personal data collection.** We do not collect personal information.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app does not use any tracking tools, analytics SDKs, or advertising services.
- **No accounts.** No registration or sign-in is required.
- **Stored on your device.** Your drawings and created worlds stay on your device and are never uploaded to us.

## Purchases and Downloads

Optional in-app purchases are handled securely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Additional World Themes may be downloaded from our content servers without requiring an account.

## Children's Privacy

DoDoDoodle is designed for young children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Policy Updates

We may update this policy when necessary. Any changes will be reflected in the date above.
