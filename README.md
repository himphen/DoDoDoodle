# DoDoDoodle

**Draw it. Bring it to life.**

DoDoDoodle is a playful drawing app for iPad. Draw your own characters, bring them to life, and explore colourful interactive worlds.

Designed for young children and families, DoDoDoodle encourages creativity, imagination, and open-ended play.
