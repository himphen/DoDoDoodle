# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 8 October 2026**

DoDoDoodle is designed with privacy in mind, especially for young children.

## Information We Collect

DoDoDoodle does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

Drawings, created worlds, and other app content are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to us.

## Purchases

DoDoDoodle may offer optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details.

The app may receive information from Apple necessary to determine whether purchased content is available to you.

## Downloadable Content

Some optional content, such as additional World Themes, may be downloaded from our content servers. These downloads do not require you to create an account or provide personal information.

## Advertising and Tracking

DoDoDoodle does not contain third-party advertising and does not track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

## Children’s Privacy

DoDoDoodle is designed to be suitable for young children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app or our privacy practices change. The latest version will be made available with an updated revision date.
